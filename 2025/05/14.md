# ✨ Reading *The Great Gatsby*

## 📖 What I Learnt from the Opening of the Novel

---

in my younger and more **vulnerable** years my father gave me some advice that I've been turning over in my mind every since.  
"Whenever you feel like criticizing any one," he told me, "just remember that all the people in this world haven't had the advantages that you've had."  
He didn't say any more, but we've always been unusually communicative in a **reserved** way, and I understood that he meant a great deal more than that. In consequence, I'm **inclined** to **reserve** all judgments, a habit that has opened up many curious natures to me and also made me the **victim** of not a few **veteran bores**.

> 在我年少而更易受影响的时候，父亲曾给过我一条建议，我从那时起就一直在脑海中反复思考。  
> 他说：“每当你想要批评任何人时，记住这个道理：这个世界上的人，并非都拥有你所拥有的那些优势。”  
> 他没有多说什么，但我们之间一直以一种克制的方式进行着不同寻常的交流，我明白他所说的远不止这些。  
> 因此，我倾向于保留所有判断——这个习惯让我接触到许多性格古怪的人，同时也让我成为不少经验丰富的无聊之人的受害者。

---

The **abnormal** mind is quick to detect and attach itself to this quality when it appears in a normal person, and so it came about that in college I was **unjustly** **accused** of being a **politician** because I was **privy** to the secret **griefs** of wild, unknown men.

> 异常的心灵一旦在一个正常人身上发现这种特质，就会迅速察觉并依附于它。  
> 于是，在大学时，我被不公正地指责为一个政客，因为我知晓那些狂野而陌生男人们的秘密悲伤。

---

Most of the confidences were **unsought** — frequently I have **feigned** sleep, **preoccupation**, or a **hostile** **levity** when I realized by some unmistakable sign that an **intimate** **revelation** was **quivering** on the horizon; for the **intimate** **revelations** of young men, or at least the terms in which they express them, are usually **plagiaristic** and **marred** by obvious **suppressions**.

> 大多数的推心置腹都是未曾请求的——我常常假装睡着、心不在焉，或摆出一种敌意的轻浮态度，  
> 当我从某些无误的迹象中察觉到，一场亲密的倾诉即将在地平线上颤抖出现；  
> 因为年轻人的亲密告白——或者至少是他们表达这些情感的方式——通常是模仿来的，而且明显夹杂着压抑和隐瞒。

---

**Reserving** judgments is a matter of infinite hope. I am still a little afraid of missing something if I forget that, as my father **snobbishly** suggested, and I **snobbishly** repeat, a sense of the fundamental **decencies** is **parceled out** unequally at birth.

> 保留判断是一种无尽希望的问题。  
> 我仍有些担心，如果我忘记了这一点——正如我父亲带着势利眼光所暗示，而我又势利地重复——那就是：  
> 基本体面的观念在人出生时就被不平等地分配了，我可能会错过什么。

---

and, after **boasting** this way of my **tolerance**, I come to the **admission** that is has a limit. **Conduct** may be founded on the hard rock or the wet marshes but after a certain point I don't care what it's founded on. When I came back from the East last autumn I felt that I wanted the world to be in **uniform** and at a sort of **moral** attention forever; I wanted no more **riotous** **excursions** with **privileged** **glimpses** into the human heart.

> 在这样夸耀自己的宽容之后，我不得不承认，它是有极限的。  
> 人的行为也许是建立在坚硬的岩石上，也许是建立在潮湿的沼泽地上，  
> 但到了某个程度，我已经不在乎它建立在什么之上了。  
> 去年秋天我从东部回来时，我感到自己希望这个世界永远保持整齐划一的姿态，永远处于某种道德上的立正状态；  
> 我再也不想经历那种喧闹放纵的冒险，也不想再窥见人类内心的那些特权式的瞬间了。

---

Only Gatsby, the man who gives his name to this book, was **exempt** from my reaction — Gatsby, who represented everything for which I have an unaffected **scorn**. If personality is an **unbroken** series of successful gestures, then there was something **gorgeous** about him, some **heightened** **sensitivity** to the promises of life, as if he were related to one of those **intricate** machines that register earthquakes ten thousand miles away.

> 只有盖茨比——这个把自己的名字赋予这本书的人——例外，没有引起我那种反感。  
> 盖茨比代表着我一向真诚鄙视的一切。  
> 如果说“个性”是一连串成功的姿态，那么他身上确实有一种迷人的气质，  
> 一种对生活承诺格外敏锐的感知，仿佛他是某种精密仪器的一部分，那种可以感应到一万英里之外地震的仪器。

---

This **responsiveness** had nothing to do with that **flabby** **impressionability** which is **dignified** under the name of the "creative **temperament**" -- it was an **extraordinary** gift for hope, a romantic **readiness** such as I have never found in any other people and <u>which it is not likely I shall ever find again</u>. No -- Gatsby turned out all right at the end; it is what **preyed** on Gatsby, what **foul** **dust** **floated** in the wake of his dreams that temporarily closed out my interest in the **abortive** **sorrows** and **short-winded** **elations** of men.

> 这种敏感并不属于那种被冠以“创造性气质”之名、实际上是软弱的易受影响性——它是一种非凡的希望天赋，
> 一种浪漫的准备好去相信的态度，我在其他人身上从未见过，我也不太可能再遇到。
> 不——盖茨比最终是没问题的；
> 真正摧毁盖茨比的，是那些潜伏在他梦想背后、污浊的尘埃，
> 它们暂时熄灭了我对人类那种无果的哀愁与气短的欢欣的兴趣。

---
